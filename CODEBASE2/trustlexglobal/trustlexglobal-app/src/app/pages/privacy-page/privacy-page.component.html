2. Information we collect

Information you give us through the Website. When you submit the consultation form, we collect your first and last name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, your message, and your preferred consultation time.

Information you give us as a client. To carry out an engagement we collect the information and documents needed for it, which may include identification documents such as a passport, residential and business addresses, details of the owners and managers of the company being formed, and information about the nature of the business.

Communications. We keep the emails, messages and call notes exchanged with you.

Technical information. The Website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Like most websites, the providers that host and deliver the Website and its fonts and icons receive standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type when your browser loads a page.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to enquiries and arrange consultations;
  • provide the services you request, including preparing and submitting filings;
  • verify identity and assess whether we can accept an engagement;
  • communicate with you about your engagement, including deadlines and renewals;
  • keep business and accounting records; and
  • comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where data protection laws such as the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation apply, we rely on the following legal bases: performance of a contract with you or steps taken at your request before entering into one; our legitimate interests in running and protecting our business; compliance with legal obligations; and your consent where we ask for it, which you may withdraw at any time.

5. Who we share information with

We share personal information only as needed to provide our services and run our business:

  • government authorities and registries that receive your filings, such as a Secretary of State office or the US Internal Revenue Service;
  • registered agents appointed for your company;
  • service providers that support our operations, such as website hosting, form processing, email and document storage providers; and
  • professional advisers, courts or authorities where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Please note that some information included in government filings becomes part of the public record.

We operate from India and form companies in the United States and other jurisdictions, and our service providers may be located in other countries. Your information may therefore be transferred to and processed in countries whose data protection laws differ from those where you live. Where required, we take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

7. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and follow up. We keep client records for the duration of the engagement and afterwards for as long as required for legal, tax and record-keeping purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
